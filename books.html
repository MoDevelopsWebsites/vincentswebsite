Books & Documentaries


Kobe Bryant was not only a legendary basketball player but also an accomplished author and filmmaker. Here are some of his notable works:

Books

  • The Mamba Mentality: How I Play - This book offers a deep look into Kobe's approach to the game, his training regimen, and his insights on the sport.
  • Legacy and the Queen - A young adult fantasy novel co-authored by Kobe Bryant and Annie Matthew, published by Granity Studios.
  • Epoca: The Tree of Ecrof - Another young adult fantasy novel co-authored by Kobe Bryant and Ivy Claire, also published by Granity Studios.

Documentaries

  • Dear Basketball - An animated short film based on a poem Kobe wrote announcing his retirement from basketball. The film won an Academy Award for Best Animated Short Film in 2018.
  • Kobe Bryant's Muse - A documentary that provides a personal look into Kobe's life, career, and inspirations.
