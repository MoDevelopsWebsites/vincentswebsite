About Kobe Bryant


Kobe Bryant was an American professional basketball player. A shooting guard, he spent his entire 20-year career with the Los Angeles Lakers in the National Basketball Association (NBA). Bryant is widely regarded as one of the greatest basketball players of all time.

Born in Philadelphia and partly raised in Italy, Bryant was recognized as the top high-school basketball player in the U.S. while at Lower Merion High School in Ardmore, Pennsylvania. The son of former NBA player Joe Bryant, he declared for the 1996 NBA draft and was selected by the Charlotte Hornets with the 13th overall pick; the Hornets then traded him to the Lakers. As a rookie, Bryant earned himself a reputation as a high-flyer and a fan favorite by winning the 1997 Slam Dunk Contest, and he was named an All-Star by his second season.

Despite a feud with teammate Shaquille O'Neal, the pair led the Lakers to three consecutive NBA championships from 2000 to 2002. In 2003, Bryant was accused of sexual assault, but the charges were eventually dropped, and a civil suit was settled out of court. Bryant denied the allegations and maintained his innocence. After the Lakers lost the 2004 NBA Finals, O'Neal was traded, and Bryant became the cornerstone of the Lakers. He led the NBA in scoring during the 2005–06 and 2006–07 seasons. In 2006, he scored a career-high 81 points, the second most points scored in a single game in league history, behind Wilt Chamberlain's 100-point game in 1962.

In 2008, Bryant was named the NBA Most Valuable Player (MVP) and led his team to the 2008 NBA Finals as the first seed in the Western Conference. He won two more championships in 2009 and 2010, earning the Finals MVP Award on both occasions. He continued to be among the top players in the league through 2013, when he suffered a torn Achilles tendon at age 34. Although he recovered, he suffered season-ending injuries to his knee and shoulder in the following two seasons. Citing physical decline, Bryant retired after the 2015–16 season.

At the time of his death, Bryant was the all-time leading scorer in Lakers franchise history, and ranks fourth on the NBA all-time regular season scoring and all-time postseason scoring lists. He was the first guard in NBA history to play at least 20 seasons. His 18 All-Star designations are the second most all time, while it is the record for most consecutive appearances as a starter. Bryant's four All-Star MVP Awards are tied with Bob Pettit for the most in NBA history. He gave himself the nickname "Black Mamba" in the mid-2000s, and the epithet became widely adopted by the general public. He won gold medals as a member of the U.S. national team in the 2008 and 2012 Summer Olympics.
